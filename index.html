What is this?

Local, community-led rides at dawn.

Inspired by sunrise ride crews around the world, East York Dawn Patrol is a weekly, no-cost road ride. We keep it simple: meet on time, ride predictably, and share the sunrise. We usually aim for a flat-to-rolling ~80 km loop and coffee at the finish.

Open to experienced road cyclists who are comfortable in a rotating paceline. This is not a coached or insured event; you ride at your own risk.

  • When: Sundays, roll at 06:30 (arrive 5 min early)
  • Where: Mortimer Ave & Lismore Crescent, East York (map)
  • Distance: ~80 km
  • Cost: Free
  • Groups: A ≈ 27 km/h · B ≈ 25 km/h
  • Requirements: Helmet, front & rear lights, drop-bar road bike, basics to fix a flat, respect the road & each other

Route & Distance

~80 km sunrise loops

Routes vary week to week based on wind and roadworks. Expect East-end classics (Lakeshore, Rouge, Pickering, Durham back-roads) or a Don Valley loop. We announce the route at the start; bring a computer if you want your own breadcrumbs.

Want to help plan routes? Bring ideas or a GPX on the day.

Start: Mortimer Ave & Lismore Crescent

Arrive with lights on. We leave on time — 06:30 sharp.

Ride rules

Safety & Etiquette

  • Wear a helmet and use front & rear lights.
  • Road bikes only. Keep bars predictable; no tri/TT bars in the group.
  • Obey traffic laws and ride single file when required. Be a good ambassador.
  • Hold your line, call hazards, and rotate smoothly at the front.
  • Carry spares & tools; you are responsible for your own bike and safety.
  • Be respectful. We’re here for a good ride and good vibes.

We leave at 06:30.

“Sharp” means sharp. Please arrive a few minutes early for a quick hello and roll-out brief.

This is an informal community ride without insurance or support. You ride at your own risk.

FAQ

Good to know

Is it really free?

Yep. It’s just folks riding bikes. No fees, no sign-ups.

What if it rains?

We’re fair-weather by default. If the roads are unsafe, common sense prevails. Decide at the start.

Can I bring friends?

Please do—if they’re comfortable with group riding at the posted paces and follow the rules.

Can I suggest a route?

Absolutely. Bring your GPX on the day or share ideas at the start.
